# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 5, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how The Creative Technician ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with our website, services, and interactions with you.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices. Please read this Policy carefully.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
We collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

- **Contact Form Data:** Name, email address, phone number, project details
- **Booking Information:** Information provided through Calendly integration
- **WhatsApp Communication:** Messages sent via our WhatsApp link
- **Email Communication:** When you email us at willardwells@gmail.com
- **Service Inquiries:** Details about your project or service needs

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
We may automatically collect:

- **Website Usage:** Pages visited, time spent, links clicked
- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Analytics Data:** Via Google Analytics (see Section 3)
- **Cookies:** Non-essential cookies only with your consent

### 2.3 Third-Party Services
We use third-party platforms that may collect data:

- **Calendly:** Booking/scheduling integration
- **Google Analytics:** Website traffic analysis
- **Email Service Providers:** For newsletter/communication distribution
- **WhatsApp Business:** For messaging integration

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- **Respond to your inquiries** and provide customer service
- **Schedule consultations and service delivery** via Calendly
- **Send service proposals and updates** (with your consent)
- **Improve our website and services** based on user behavior
- **Comply with legal obligations** and protect against fraud
- **Market our services** (only if you opt-in)

**We do not sell your personal data to third parties.**

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR/Data Privacy)

We process your data based on:
- **Contract:** To provide services you've requested
- **Consent:** When you explicitly agree
- **Legitimate Interest:** To improve our services and protect our business
- **Legal Compliance:** To meet regulatory requirements

## 5. Data Retention

- **Contact inquiries:** Retained for 2 years or until service completion
- **Project files/deliverables:** Retained per service agreement (typically 1-3 years)
- **Email lists:** Retained until unsubscribed
- **Analytics data:** Retained per Google Analytics default (26 months)
- **Cookies:** Deleted when you clear browser cookies or after 12 months of inactivity

### 6.2 Correction & Deletion
You can request corrections to inaccurate data or deletion of your information, subject to legal requirements.

### 6.3 Opt-Out

- **Analytics:** Opt-out via Google Analytics opt-out extension
