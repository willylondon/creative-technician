​Life at the intersection of IT Support and Content Creation is never dull. One minute I’m deep in the Google Workspace admin console troubleshooting Chromebooks for the school day, and the next, I’m scouting the best spots in Kingston for my next food review.
​People often ask how I manage the "Creative Tech" lifestyle. The secret? Automation. Whether it's using Google Apps Script to streamline school workflows or setting up n8n to manage my social media schedule, tech is the engine that keeps my creative side running. It’s what gives me the time to hit the trails at Nonsuch Falls or catch a JamRockerz game without missing a beat.
​My Top 3 Efficiency Hacks:
​Script Everything: If you have to do it twice, automate it.
​Stay Mobile: A solid remote setup is key when you're moving between the office and the streets.
​Refuel Right: You can't debug on an empty stomach!
