If you haven’t been paying attention to Jamaica on the international basketball stage — now’s the time. The Reggae Boyz of hoops are deep into the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup Americas Qualifiers, and the calendar is bringing some huge home games that fans won’t want to miss. 

Jamaica’s national team is battling it out to secure a spot in the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup in Qatar — one of the biggest stages in world basketball. 

🔜 Big Games on the Schedule
• Feb 26, 2026 — Jamaica vs Bahamas in the FIBA World Cup Americas Qualifiers at the National Indoor Sports Centre in Jamaica. That game is set to tip off in late February and is a must-watch as the Jamaicans look to rack up home-court wins. 
• Mar 1, 2026 — Jamaica faces Canada in another qualifier matchup that promises high-level competition and intense Caribbean pride on the line.
